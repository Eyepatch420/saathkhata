Privacy Policy — SaathKhata

Last updated: August 2026
In short: SaathKhata is a shared ledger app connecting vendors and customers to track credit and payments. We collect only what's needed to run your account and ledger, we never sell your data, and your ledger entries are only shared with the vendor/customer you transact with.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, mobile number, UPI ID
  • Profile photo (optional, stored via Cloudinary)
  • Transaction data: ledger entries, payment records
  • Device token, used to send push notifications
  • Location, only when you use the location picker feature

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the SaathKhata service
  • To send transaction notifications and payment reminders
  • To generate reports and summaries for your account
  • To improve the app and fix issues

3. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your ledger data is shared only with the linked vendor or customer you transact with.

4. Data security

We use industry-standard security measures including bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication, and HTTPS-only communication.

5. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion by contacting us (see Section 10, or use in-app Settings → Delete Account).

6. Push notifications

You can opt out of push notifications at any time through your device settings.

7. Third-party services

We use Cloudinary for profile photo storage and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. These services have their own privacy policies.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us using the details in Section 10 to exercise these rights.

9. Children's privacy

SaathKhata is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or to request account/data deletion, contact us at info@igurus.in or call +91 90759 69534.
