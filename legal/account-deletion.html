Delete Your Account — SaathKhata

SaathKhata (developer: Igurus) lets you request deletion of your account and all associated data.

How to request account deletion

  • Open the SaathKhata app, go to Settings → Delete Account, and confirm the deletion request.
  • If you no longer have access to the app, email info@igurus.in or call +91 90759 69534 from your registered contact details and request account deletion.

Requests are processed within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile information: name, email, mobile number, UPI ID, profile photo
  • Your login credentials
  • Device tokens used for push notifications

What may be retained

  • Ledger and transaction records shared with a linked vendor/customer may be retained by the other party as part of their own business records, since these are jointly-owned transaction entries.
  • Records we are legally required to retain (e.g. for fraud prevention or tax/compliance purposes) may be kept for up to 90 days after deletion, then permanently removed.
For questions, contact info@igurus.in
